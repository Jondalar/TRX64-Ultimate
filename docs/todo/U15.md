# U15: Register traces of real tunes

Status 📋. Open (one line, as in the TODO table): Source: the c64re runtime (Alex 2026-11-14); first trace (Mahoney, Musik Run/Stop) plays through `tb_cmp` and `filt_cmp`; the exporter is not in the repo yet

## Description

Split off from [U12](../specs/U12-the-sound-of-a-6581.md) (§5 Q6, Q1 answered by Alex 2026-11-14). `tb_wav` (U12
D5) and `filt_cmp` (U12 D1e) play a register log in the format `cores/trx_sid/model/gen_logs.py` writes
(`<idle> W <reg> <val>`, hex). To hear or measure *Meanwhile the Planet*, Mechanicus or F20, something has to
turn a `.sid` into that log. Candidates: `siddump` (external, the reviewer's own route), VICE, or our c64re
runtime, which already stores bus events; the last costs no new dependency. Which one is Alex's call.

## Status and history

- **2026-11-14, numbered.** Alex agreed to U12's scope (Q1): register traces are a tool of their own.
- **2026-11-14, source: the c64re runtime.** Why not the others: `siddump` has a 6502 and no C64 around it (no
  CIA, no NMI) and prints the registers once per frame, so a digi player that writes `$D418` from timer interrupts
  every ~22 cycles is lost; VICE would work but is a dependency. Which SID emulation the tracer has does not matter:
  only the writes and their cycles are taken. Alex: *"Dann versuch das"*.
- **2026-11-14, first trace.** Mahoney's *Musik Run/Stop* (HVSC `MUSICIANS/M/Mahoney/Musik_Run_Stop.sid`, RSID,
  init `$0800`, play `$0000`) as a PRG in a c64re sandbox (PAL), PC set to `$0800`, a `memory`-domain trace for
  10 s (the `sid` domain recorded nothing). An exporter (trial, in the session scratchpad) keeps the accesses to
  `$D400-$D7FF` while I/O is visible (from the trace's own writes to `$01`, which the player switches between `$34`
  and `$35`; 1 934 accesses with I/O hidden dropped) and writes `gen_logs.py`'s format: 239 075 writes in 9.6 M
  cycles. The init writes every register (voices AD/SR `$FF`, control `$49`, cutoff `$7FF`, `$D417` = `$03`), then
  `$D418` follows every 22 cycles (21/23/20 at the edges). Through reDIP (`tb_cmp`, hardware build) against
  libresidfp (`filt_cmp`, `EXTFILT=1`, 2 M cycles): correlation 0.675 as released, 0.686 with U12's node - the
  baseline U12 D4 starts from.
- Open: the exporter into the repo, `.sid` to PRG and the start in one command, the other tunes of U12, all in the local
  HVSC: *Meanwhile the Planet* (`MUSICIANS/T/TBB/Meanwhile_the_Planet.sid`, 6581), Mechanicus
  (`MUSICIANS/M/Mueller_Markus/Mechanicus.sid`, 6581, song 3 of 18) and F20 (`MUSICIANS/V/Vincenzo/F20.sid`, 8580).
